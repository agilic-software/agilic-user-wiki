# Section 4 \- Teams

Section 4 covers Teams \- standing groups of people separate from any single Work Package, with their own roster, work, reporting, and timesheet. This is where team structure and ongoing team-level admin live.

## Contents of this Section:

* [**Navigate a Team**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s4-teams/navigate-a-team) \- A full tour of every screen in a Team \- roster, work, reporting, and timesheet. Start here.  
* [**Create a Team**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s4-teams/create-a-team) \- Creating a new team and keeping its membership current, from Org Admin or from within My Team if you're that team's Owner or Driver.  
* [**Team Management**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s4-teams/team-management) \- A functional group of people who do similar work or work collectively across an Organization, sharing the same Owner/Driver. My Team closely mirrors My View, scoped to your team.
