# Section 1 \- Agilic Fundamentals

The Agilic Fundamentals are where a brand-new user starts. It orients you to the parts of Agilic you'll touch every day, gives you the core vocabulary the rest of the wiki assumes you already know, and lays out what each role can actually do. If you're new, read these three pages in order before jumping into any section-specific guide.

## Contents of this Section:

For a Playlist of most of the contents below: 

* [**Core Terms & Concepts**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s1-agilic-fundamentals/core-terms-concepts) \- Defines the building blocks used throughout the rest of the wiki: Work Packages (WPs), Items, Milestones, and the four native Work Item Types (Define, Do Work, Document, Deliver \- the "4D's") plus RIDE Items. Start here \- it's very brief.  
  * For an under 4 minute Playlist explaining the Core Terms & Concepts: [https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLRPBZu7dC3ln-UNEV491FbYhfm-rYcuPY](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLRPBZu7dC3ln-UNEV491FbYhfm-rYcuPY)  
      
* [**Navigate as a User**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s1-agilic-fundamentals/navigate-as-a-user) \- A tour of the persistent top navigation, the Left-Hand Menu, Quick-Access Pill Buttons, Home and Private Work Package buttons, and Custom Links.   
* [**Roles & Permissions**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s1-agilic-fundamentals/roles-permissions) \- "I'm a \[role\] \- what can I actually do in Agilic?" A quick-reference table of what each role level (Every User, Assigned/Responsible, WP Roster Member, WP Owner/Driver) can see and do, with links out to the authoritative detail in other sections.
