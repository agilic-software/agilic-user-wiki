# Appendix

The Appendix holds Agilic's cross-referenced reference material \- a single-term glossary and a feature-grouped index \- useful once you already know the section content and just need to look something up quickly.

## Contents of this Section:

* [**Agilic Terminology Index & Dictionary**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s-appendix/terminology-index) \- The authoritative single-term glossary. Each entry gives a short definition and points to the section(s) where it's covered in full detail.  
* [**Feature Index**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s-appendix/feature-index) \- Groups related terms into feature areas (Work Package Basics, Reporting & Visualization, Planning & Scheduling, and more) and points to the primary how-to document for each. Expands on the Terminology Index rather than replacing it.
