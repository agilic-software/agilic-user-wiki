# Section 7 \- Timesheets

Section 7 covers Agilic's Timesheets \- both Allocation Management for Pre-Planning and Logging Time for recording actual hours worked.

## Contents of this Section:

* [**Timesheets**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s7-timesheets/timesheets) \- Agilic Timesheets have two functions: Allocation Management, to help with Pre-Planning by reserving capacity ahead of time, and Logging Time, to record actual hours worked.
