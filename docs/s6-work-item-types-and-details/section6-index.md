# Section 6 \- Work Item Types and Details

Section 6 covers the actual day-to-day work inside a Work Package: the four Work Item Types (Define, Do Work, Document, Deliver), the shared item detail structure they all use, and the related fifth type, RIDE.

## Contents of this Section:

* [**Work Item Types**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s6-work-item-types-and-details/work-item-types) \- Define, Do Work, Document, and Deliver \- the four working tabs of Agilic's 4D Framework, plus RIDE Items where most of your work outside your plan actually happens.  
* [**Working Standard Items**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s6-work-item-types-and-details/working-standard-items) \- Everything you can capture on a single item, whether it lives in Define, Document, Do Work, or Deliver. Learn the shared item detail structure once \- it applies everywhere.  
* [**Working RIDE Items**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s6-work-item-types-and-details/working-ride-items) \- RIDE stands for Risks, Issues, Dependencies, and Escalations \- the unforeseen work that comes up through a project's lifecycle. Covers the optional RIDE-specific processes on top of the standard item detail \- like Mitigation and Decision-Making.
