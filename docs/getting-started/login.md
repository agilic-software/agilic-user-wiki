# Signing In

The login screen is how you access your Agilic account. From here you can sign in with your
email and password, sign in with Google, create a new account, or reset a forgotten password.

<!-- screenshot: add an image of the login screen here -->

## Arriving via an invite

If you followed an invitation link from someone at another organisation, you'll see a banner
above the sign-in form showing that organisation's logo and name, letting you know who invited
you before you sign in.

## Signing in with email and password

1. Enter your **email** address.
2. Enter your **password**. Use the eye icon to show or hide what you've typed.
3. Confirm that you agree to the **Terms & Conditions** and **Privacy Policy** (tap either
   link to read them).
4. Click **Sign In**.

### If something's wrong with your details

- **Email is required.** — you left the email field empty.
- **Email is not valid.** — what you entered doesn't look like a valid email address.
- **Password is required.** — you left the password field empty.

Fix the highlighted field and click **Sign In** again.

## Signing in with Google

Instead of using a password, you can sign in with your Google account. Under **Or Continue
with**, click the Google button and follow the Google sign-in prompts.

## New user?

If you don't have an account yet, click **Register** next to "New User?" to create one.

## Forgot your password?

Click **Reset password** to start the password recovery process.

## First-time user?

Click **Read me** for a quick introduction to getting started with Agilic.

## Your privacy

Click **Do Not Share Personal Information** to learn what information you should avoid
entering into the app.
