# Section 2 \- Setting Up Your Org

Section 2 covers everything involved in standing up and administering an Agilic organization \- from the very first account setup, through configuring org-wide settings and templates, to the recurring admin work of managing people and billing. If you're an Org Admin, this is your home base.

## Contents of this Section:

* [**Navigate Org Admin**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s2-setting-up-your-org/navigate-org-admin) \- A quick tour of the Org Admin console \- separate from the everyday work areas \- and what each tab is for. Start here if you're new to Org Admin.  
* [**Create Your Organization**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s2-setting-up-your-org/create-your-organization) \- Walks through creating a brand-new Agilic account and setting up your organization for the first time. For the very first person from your company to sign up.  
* [**Things to Consider When Setting Up Your Org**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s2-setting-up-your-org/things-to-consider-when-setting-up-your-org) \- The companion "what" and "why" to Setting Up Org Templates \- a plain-English guide (with exercises) to deciding your Item States, WP Phases, and Labels before you enter them.  
  * This is a very worthwhile section to review while setting up your Organization.  
      
* [**Setting Up Org Templates**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s2-setting-up-your-org/setting-up-org-templates) \- A hands-on, click-by-click walkthrough for entering the Item States, WP Phases, and Labels you've already decided on into Agilic. This is the "how."  
  * The How To companion of 'Things to Consider When Setting Up Your Org'

* [**Org Settings**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s2-setting-up-your-org/org-settings) \- The handful of org-wide settings worth configuring early, before your team starts creating Work Packages.  
* [**Permissions for People**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s2-setting-up-your-org/permissions-for-people) \- The different levels of access a person can have in Agilic, and where each one is controlled. Permissions aren't a single on/off switch \- they're a combination of several separate flags and roles.  
* [**Adding & Managing People**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s2-setting-up-your-org/adding-and-managing-people) \- The ongoing admin task of inviting new people into your organization, assigning them permissions, and keeping the People list current. Something you'll come back to regularly.  
* [**Managing Billing**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s2-setting-up-your-org/managing-billing) \- Your organization's subscription and AI usage tracking. Only the person flagged as Billing Admin can see this area.
