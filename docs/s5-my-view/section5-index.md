# Section 5 \- My View

Section 5 covers My View \- your personal dashboard, pulling together everything relevant to you across every Work Package you're part of, plus your Private Work Package, Profile, and Settings.

## Contents of this Section:

* [**My View Overview**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s5-my-view/my-view-overview) \- The connector document for My View \- ties together the first-day tour and points to the dedicated pages for each area. Start here.  
* [**My Work**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s5-my-view/my-work) \- A tabbed view of items relevant to you, pulled from across all your Work Packages.  
* [**My Private Work Package**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s5-my-view/my-private-work-package) \- A summary-level overview of Private Work Packages \- what they are and how they're identified.  
* [**My Timesheet**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s5-my-view/my-timesheet) \- All your allocations and logged hours within every Work Package you're part of.  
* [**My View Reporting**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s5-my-view/my-view-reporting) \- Everything under My View's Reporting tab, working like Work Package-level reports but pulling data across every Work Package you're part of.  
* [**My Profile**](https://agilic-wiki.local/s5-my-view/my-profile) \- Your personal account area \- account details and additional personal information.
